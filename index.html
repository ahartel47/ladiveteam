Why we exist

One coast. Thousands of divers. One team.

From the leap off the stairs at Casino Point to a Saturday shore entry at Vets Park, Los Angeles divers all share the same water — but we mostly dive in small, separate circles. Shops, clubs, buddy pairs, solo regulars who ask how the vis was when they pass each other in the parking lot.

LA Dive Team exists to make one community out of many: divers who show up for each other, for the ocean, and for the next generation getting in the water. We want LA to dive more — and to dive like it matters.

Not just more dives. Remove waste, catalogue endangered marine life, educate youth, find new dive buddies. Our mission

Programs

What we do between surface intervals

Six ways in. Come for any of them — you'll end up at the others anyway.

Beach cleanup days

Regular cleanups on the beaches we launch from, above and below the waterline — because every dive starts on the sand, and the sand is where the trash starts too.

Community dives

Recreational dives for every level, from first ocean dive to hundredth night dive. Show up solo; leave with a buddy list.

Educating The Next Generation

Bringing the underwater world to underprivileged LA kids — pool sessions, snorkel days, and pathways to certification for young people who'd never otherwise get the chance.

Citizen science

Divers are the ocean's field researchers. We collect survey data on our dives and put it in the hands of the conservation groups fighting for this coast.

Marine life advocacy

Spreading the word on endangered locals — white abalone, giant sea bass — and what it actually takes to bring them back to our reefs.

Social events

Post-dive breakfasts, gear swaps, film nights, bonfires. The community half of "dive community" — where dive buddies become actual friends.

Get involved

The team part is you

We're just getting started, and the founding crew shapes everything. Three ways in:

Dive with us

Join the mailing list and come to the first cleanup, dive day, or social. Every certification level welcome — and so are snorkelers and not-yet-divers.

Volunteer

Instructors, divemasters, organizers, photographers, people who own folding tables. A new nonprofit runs on exactly these people.

Partner with us

Dive shops, clubs, conservation groups, youth organizations: let's put something on the calendar together.
